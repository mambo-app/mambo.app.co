05 / THE BONUS FEATURES

THERE'S MORE
after the credits.

All the little things that make a very big difference to your watching life.

One app. Three modes. What's the difference?

Movies gets cinematic logging and rewatch history. Series adds seasons, episode checklists, and progress. Anime brings its own discovery universe and episode-first tracking. Every mode changes MAMBO's accent, atmosphere, artwork, and metadata. Try the mode switch anywhere on this page.

Can I bring my Letterboxd history?

MAMBO's product blueprint includes import from your official Letterboxd ZIP export: watched titles, ratings, reviews, diary dates, and rewatches. The public website preview does not upload or process your archive. Your history stays with you until the connected app is available.

What about stats, release dates, and privacy?

The MAMBO app is designed for watch-time analytics, rating distributions, streaks, achievement badges, and an upcoming-release calendar with streaming providers. Granular visibility controls are planned for your library, favorites, activity, and watch history. These are product features, not live services in this website demo.

Is this the app, or an interactive preview?

This is a working, front-end experience of the MAMBO universe. You can save titles into local folders, rate them, track the included seasons, edit your Top 4, try chat attachments, export a review story card, and explore the 3D lounge. Data stays in this browser. Chat replies are simulated; there is no sign-in, live messaging, streaming, or connection to an app backend.

Does the 3D experience work on my phone?

Yes. Drag with one finger to orbit the lounge, use the labeled camera views, or sit back for a guided tour. Motion is reduced when your device requests it, and the rest of the site stays usable if WebGL is unavailable. Ambient audio is always off until you turn it on.
